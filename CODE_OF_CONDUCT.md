# Code Of Conduct

CodePipeline is a collaborative project. We want contributors, maintainers, and users to feel safe asking questions, sharing ideas, and offering feedback.

## Our Expectations

- Be respectful and assume good intent
- Give feedback about ideas and code without attacking people
- Keep discussions constructive, especially when disagreeing
- Make room for contributors with different experience levels
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, and personal attacks

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language
- Deliberate humiliation or repeated hostile behavior
- Sharing private information without permission
- Trolling, baiting, or derailing project discussions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or limit participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it through the contact method listed in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) or open a private maintainer conversation where possible.
